Reading a Deuces Wild paytable

Eight numbers, and only the middle ones move. Full-pay Deuces Wild is 25-15-9-5-3-2-2-1 at 100.76%, and the row to check first is four of a kind: it lands about once every fifteen hands.

Full-pay machines and why they became hard to find

Full pay is the top of a known ladder, not a compliment: 9/6 Jacks or Better returns 99.54%. A coin off the full house costs 1.15 points and a coin off the flush 1.10 — exactly how often those hands land.

Bankroll and session length: how far a fixed stack actually goes

The house edge says a 1,000-coin stack covers 43,800 hands. Half of real sessions at that stack are over inside 6,540. Session length is set by the ratio of stack to bet, and by nothing else.

How video poker differs from poker at a table

Same 52 cards, same hand rankings, and almost nothing else in common. The machine holds no cards, so hand strength is absolute, there is no fold, and all 2,598,960 deals get played out.

Machines that pay on kings instead of jacks

Moving the minimum paying pair from jacks to kings halves how often it lands — 13.00% of deals becomes 6.50% — and takes about ten points off the return. That is why these machines carry a joker.

2 October 2026

Why a kicker never helps

Keeping an ace alongside your trips or your pair costs between 0.11 and 0.60 coins a hand, and the loss is the same whichever high card you keep. Holding one every time it is available gives up 5.59 points of return.

1 October 2026

Why an inside straight draw is worth exactly half an outside one

Eight cards complete an open-ended four-card straight and four complete a gutshot, so one is worth 0.68 coins and the other 0.34. A gutshot with no high cards in it is worth less than throwing all five cards away.

Five Play: what multi-hand does to your bankroll, and what it doesn't

Multi-hand changes neither the return nor the correct holds. It splits one stake across independent draws, which cuts the swing per coin by 55% on a drawing hand and by nothing at all on a pat one.

Why the royal flush is worth two percentage points of the return

One hand in 40,000 pays 800 per coin. That single row carries 1.98 of the 99.54% return on 9/6 Jacks or Better, and the machine you actually experience returns 97.6%.

What variance actually means at a video poker machine

Return says where a machine ends up; variance says how far it wanders on the way. Over 1,000 hands of 9/6 Jacks or Better the likely swing is thirty times the expected loss.
