A blackjack game is a perfect thing to have on your phone for a flight, a subway ride or a waiting room with no signal. Each hand takes a few seconds, you can stop at any time, and you do not need anyone else to play. The catch is that many free blackjack apps on Google Play need a connection for chips, ads or log-in, and quietly stop working offline. This guide is a checklist for finding one that really works with no wifi and no mobile data, and that plays real 21 rules.

1. It deals with airplane mode on

The only reliable test: install the game, open it once while online (some games download data on the first launch), then switch on airplane mode and play five hands. A real offline blackjack game keeps dealing, keeps your chip balance and saves your progress on the phone. If it shows a spinner, an error or "connect to continue", it is an online game with an offline label.

2. Standard blackjack rules

Check the in-game rules page or the felt for these:

  • Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Some games, like some casinos, pay 6 to 5, which costs you more than any other rule.
  • The dealer stands on 17, or at least the game tells you whether the dealer hits soft 17.
  • All the player moves: hit, stand, double down, split, insurance and ideally surrender.
  • A shuffled deck you can trust. If the game changes the deal (some give a boost when you are losing), it should say so openly and let you switch it off.

If the rules match a real table, what you learn carries over: basic strategy works the same way in the app as in a casino.

3. Cards you can actually read

A phone screen is small, and many blackjack apps shrink the cards to fit a busy casino scene. Look at the store screenshots: can you read the card values at a glance? Are the hand totals shown as numbers, so you do not have to add up every card? Large cards and clear totals make a big difference on a train or for anyone who finds small print tiring. More on easy-to-read blackjack for older players.

4. Free chips that do not run out

Every free blackjack game uses play chips. A fair one gives you a daily bonus and a way to get going again when you run out, without forcing a purchase. Watching an ad for extra chips is fine if it is your choice. Remember that play chips have no cash value, and a good game says so clearly.

5. Ads that stay out of the way

Free games are paid for with ads. What matters is when they appear. Ads between hands are normal; ads in the middle of a hand, or ones that block the buttons, are not. Offline, most ads simply do not load, which is one more reason offline blackjack is pleasant to play.

6. Something to learn from

If you are new to 21, look for a tutorial or a rules page inside the game. Our how to play blackjack guide covers the rules on one page.
