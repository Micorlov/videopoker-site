Deuces Wild is the most popular wild-card video poker game. All four 2s act as any card you need, which makes big hands far more common, so the paytable starts at three of a kind and a pair pays nothing. Strategy that works in Jacks or Better will lose money here. The good news: Deuces Wild strategy is easiest to learn if you sort every hand by how many deuces it has.

Step 1: count your deuces

Find the list for the number of 2s in your hand and keep the first line your five cards match. Hold the deuces in every case: you never throw a wild card away.

Four deuces

  • Hold the four deuces. That is 200 per coin already; the fifth card doesn't matter.

Three deuces

  1. A wild royal flush or five of a kind: keep the whole hand.
  2. Otherwise hold just the three deuces and draw two.

Three deuces plus a pair is five of a kind, so stand. Three deuces plus a 10-J of the same suit is not a royal yet; drop the 10-J and keep the deuces.

Two deuces

  1. Four of a kind or better: keep it.
  2. Four to a royal flush: draw one.
  3. Four to a straight flush with two consecutive natural cards, 6-7 or higher (6-7, 7-8 ... up to 9-10): draw one.
  4. Otherwise hold only the two deuces.

A made flush or straight with two deuces is worse than keeping the deuces alone. It pays 2; two deuces drawn to are worth more on average.

One deuce

  1. A straight flush or better, or four of a kind.
  2. Four to a royal flush.
  3. Full house.
  4. Four to a straight flush with three consecutive natural cards, 5-6-7 or higher.
  5. Three of a kind, a straight or a flush.
  6. Any other four to a straight flush.
  7. Three to a royal flush.
  8. Three to a straight flush with two consecutive natural cards, 6-7 or higher.
  9. Otherwise hold the deuce alone and draw four.

No deuces

  1. A natural royal flush.
  2. Four to a royal flush.
  3. Any paying hand: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind.
  4. Four to a straight flush.
  5. Two pair: keep one pair and draw three.
  6. One pair.
  7. Three to a royal flush.
  8. Four to a flush.
  9. Four to an outside straight (open at both ends).
  10. Three to a straight flush.
  11. Otherwise discard everything and draw five.

Worked examples

  • 2♣ 2♦ 8♥ 9♥ K♠: two deuces, and 8-9 is not four to a straight flush. Hold just the deuces.
  • 2♠ 5♥ 5♦ 9♣ 9♠: one deuce with two pair makes a full house. Keep all five.
  • 4♥ 4♣ J♣ J♦ 7♠: no deuces, two pair. Two pair pays nothing here, so keep one pair (either) and draw three.
  • 2♥ 10♠ J♠ Q♠ 3♦: one deuce plus three to a royal is four to a royal. Draw one.

The mistakes players make most

  • Keeping a high pair "because it pays". It doesn't in Deuces Wild. Any pair is just a pair.
  • Keeping two pair. Hold one pair: you have the same chance to make trips or better, and fewer dead cards.
  • Settling for a straight or flush with two deuces. The deuces alone are worth more.
  • Holding a kicker with deuces. A random high card next to your wild cards only blocks better draws.
