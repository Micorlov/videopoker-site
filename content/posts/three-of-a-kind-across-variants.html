Three of a kind is the hand most players treat as settled: a decent made hand, hold it, move on. In video poker that is only half right. The hand pays the same 3 coins on three of the four machines in the app and 1 on the fourth, and the value of holding it swings far more than the pay column suggests, because the real prize is the draw to four of a kind.

What it pays on each machine

On the full-pay tables, per coin bet:

  • Jacks or Better (9/6): 3. Two pair pays 2, a straight 4.
  • Bonus Poker: 3. Two pair pays 2, a straight 4.
  • Double Bonus: 3. Two pair is cut to 1, so three of a kind pays three times two pair.
  • Deuces Wild: 1. It is the lowest paying hand on the table. A straight and a flush both pay 2, a full house 3.

The Deuces Wild line looks odd. In ordinary poker three of a kind outranks both a straight and a flush. Here the paytable pays a straight twice as much, because pay follows how often a hand turns up, not its poker rank. With four wild cards, three of a kind is by far the commonest finished hand: roughly 28% of hands end there, against about 7.4% on 9/6 Jacks or Better. That is why the table can only afford to pay 1. For the same reason it makes up about 28% of the full-pay Deuces Wild return, and about a fifth of the return on Jacks or Better. The detail of the other rows is in reading a Deuces Wild paytable.

Being dealt it is rare

Three of a kind as a natural five-card deal happens 54,912 times in 2,598,960 hands: 13 ranks × 4 ways to choose three suits × 66 choices of two different ranks for the side cards (from the other 12) × 4 × 4 suits for those two cards. That is 2.11%, or about once in 47 hands. Most of the three of a kind you collect on Jacks or Better arrives on the draw, which is why holding a pair sits so near the top of the strategy list.

Hold the three, throw away the kickers

On every machine here, dealt three of a kind with two unrelated side cards, you keep the three and draw two. The sums are short. After you discard, 47 cards are left, and there are 1,081 ways to draw two of them. Of those:

  • 46 contain the fourth card of your rank, so you make four of a kind;
  • 66 are a pair that makes a full house (ten ranks untouched, plus the two kicker ranks with three cards left each: 10 × 6 + 2 × 3);
  • 969 do neither, and you stay on three of a kind.

On 9/6 Jacks or Better the value of that draw is (46 × 25 + 66 × 9 + 969 × 3) / 1,081, which is about 4.30 coins per coin bet. Holding a kicker as well and drawing one card is worse: (1 × 25 + 3 × 9 + 43 × 3) / 47 is about 3.85.

The same hold, many different values

Swap in the other paytables and the identical hold changes value. In Bonus Poker and Double Bonus the four-of-a-kind row depends on rank, so which three you hold matters as well.

Three aces on Double Bonus is worth (46 × 160 + 66 × 10 + 969 × 3) / 1,081 ≈ 10.11, more than three times the 3 coins the made hand pays. Three 5s to kings on Bonus Poker is worth 4.24, slightly below Jacks or Better at 4.30, because Bonus Poker pays 8 for the full house instead of 9.

The Deuces Wild figure comes out differently, at 2.02, but for the opposite reason: the four deuces are in the 47 cards. Of the 1,081 draws, 6 hold two deuces and 4 hold one deuce plus your fourth card, all making five of a kind for 15; another 168 + 42 make four of a kind for 5 by a deuce or the natural fourth card; 60 make a full house for 3; the remaining 801 stay at 1. Total: 2,181 / 1,081 ≈ 2.02. The made hand pays 1, so the draw is worth double it. If a deuce is already in your hand the situation is different, and that is covered in the Deuces Wild strategy post.

These are full-pay values. A table that pays less for a full house or four of a kind lowers each figure, so check the paytable on screen before leaning on a number from this page.

What to take from it

  1. Three of a kind is a 3-coin hand on Jacks or Better, Bonus Poker and Double Bonus, and a 1-coin hand on Deuces Wild.
  2. Always keep the three and draw two. Keeping a kicker costs about 0.45 of a coin on Jacks or Better.
  3. On Bonus Poker and Double Bonus, aces and low trips are worth much more to hold than middle ranks because their quads pay more. See Bonus Poker and Double Bonus.
  4. On Deuces Wild expect it to be your most common result. It is the baseline, not a reward.
