What this website collects

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It records the pages you view, roughly where in the world you are, and what kind of device and browser you used. It also counts clicks on the Google Play buttons, so we can see which pages lead people to an app. It does not record your name, your email address, or anything that identifies you personally, and the data is not used for advertising.

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to recognise repeat visits. If you would rather not be counted, browser settings that block cookies or tracking scripts will stop it, as will Google’s own opt-out add-on. Nothing on the site stops working if you do.

There are no accounts, forms or comments on this website, so nothing else is collected. If you email us, your message and address are used only to reply.
