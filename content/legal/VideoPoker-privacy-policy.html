Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Video Poker: Jacks or Better (com.micorlov.videopoker)

This game is made by Michael Orlov, trading as Orlov Games, in Israel. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions go to micorlov@gmail.com.

The short version: your chips and progress stay on your device. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and shows Google AdMob ads. Signing in with Google is optional and only needed for the Friends tab.

This website

Separately from the game, this website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It records the pages you view, roughly where in the world you are, and what kind of device and browser you used. It does not record your name, your email address, or anything that identifies you personally, and the data is not used for advertising.

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to recognise repeat visits. If you would rather not be counted, browser settings that block cookies or tracking scripts will stop it, as will Google’s own opt-out add-on. Nothing on the site stops working if you do.

Everything below describes the Android app.

Information We Collect

  • Gameplay data stored on your device: your chip balance, hand statistics, settings and progress are saved on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers and are not backed up by us.
  • Account information (only if you sign in): signing in is optional and is only needed for the Friends tab. If you sign in with Google, we receive your display name, email address, profile photo and a unique account identifier.
  • Friends and invites (only if you sign in): your invite code, the connections between you and the friends you add, today's net chip profit (shown to your friends on the daily leaderboard), and records of invite rewards.
  • Invite links: if you install the app from a friend's invite link, Google Play passes us the invite code in the install referrer so we can connect you with that friend.
  • Analytics and device identifiers: we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. It collects app events (for example, app opened, onboarding step, hand played, daily bonus claimed), device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version), an app instance identifier, your Android advertising ID, and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising (Android app): Google AdMob shows a banner ad and occasional full-screen ads between hands. AdMob collects your Android advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required by law, including in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices.
  • Unity Ads (advertising partner): some of the ads that AdMob shows are served by Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies. Unity Ads collects your Android advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, how you interact with ads, and purchase signals, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The same consent choices apply. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily reminder on your device. We do not collect a push notification token.

Advertising ID

The app has access to your Android advertising ID. It is used for analytics through Google Analytics for Firebase and by Google AdMob to show and measure ads, which may be personalized depending on your choices and location. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not process real-money payments or financial account information.
  • We do not collect precise location data.

How We Use Data

Data is used to operate the game's features (the Friends tab, invites and invite rewards), to understand and improve how the app is used, to show ads, and to prevent invite rewards from being claimed twice.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Analytics) as our service provider; your data is processed by Google on our behalf. Google AdMob and its advertising partners receive the advertising data described above; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you sign in, your friends can see your display name and today's net chip profit.

Unity Technologies receives the advertising data described above when Unity Ads serves an ad; see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

If you signed in, you can delete your account in the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, your friend connections and your daily score from our servers, and signs you out. Chips and progress stored on your device are not affected; uninstalling the app removes them.

You can also request deletion without the app on our account deletion page, or by contacting micorlov@gmail.com.

Invite-reward records (the account identifier, display name and reward status) are kept after deletion so that a reward cannot be claimed twice. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics according to its retention settings and is not linked to your account.

No Real Money Gambling

This game is intended for entertainment purposes only. It does not offer real money gambling or the ability to win real money or prizes.

Children's Privacy

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Analytics and ad measurement in those regions start only after you consent; withdraw in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices, or turn off Settings → Share usage statistics. To exercise a right, email micorlov@gmail.com, or use the account deletion page.

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 13 (or the applicable local age of digital consent).

Changes to This Policy
